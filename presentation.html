Why change staking at all?

Why · 1 of 4

Cost outruns value captured.

The protocol earns almost nothing. It pays out ~70M DOT/yr in rewards and treasury. Most of that gets sold for fiat — leaves the DOT economy.

Why · 2 of 4

Security tracks price. Price has slipped.

An attacker needs ⅓ of validators — the cheapest path to halt finality. Each slot costs the smallest active backing (~1.28M DOT today). The dollar cost moves with DOT price.

Why · 4 of 4

Where we are. Where we want to be.

What we have
  • Cost outruns value. ~76k DOT/yr in. ~70M DOT/yr out.
  • Security tracks DOT price. $339M to attack today. $25.5B at peak.
  • We overpay nominators. ~92M DOT in 2025 to back 462 zero-self-stake validators.
What we want
  • Reduce overall cost of securing the network.
  • Lower nominator APY — pay less for curation we don't get.
  • Use the savings: more to validators (skin-in-the-game), more to buffer.
  • Pay validator infra costs in fiat — when DOT goes up, our cost stays flat.
  • Build savings for when issuance shrinks further.

What · 3

Role changes.

Validators
  • 10k DOT min self-stake — enforced end of May 2026.
  • Slashable on the validator's own bond, as before.
  • Higher APY via the new validator_incentive pot — target 70% on 30k self-stake.
  • Infra costs in pUSD — Q4 2026, paid in stables instead of DOT.
Nominators
  • Unslashable — no longer carry validator risk.
  • 2-day unbond — replaces the 28-day lock.
  • Lower APY — target ~3%.
  • Same role: point stake at validators they trust.
